Interior Design

The cabin of the Lexus IS is crafted around the driver, creating a focused and intuitive driving environment.

A driver-centric cockpit places essential controls within easy reach, supported by a large dual 12.3-inch display that presents information clearly and efficiently.

Every detail has been thoughtfully designed to enhance comfort and engagement — from refined interior materials and wood accents to a steering wheel shaped for natural hand positioning.

Performance

Engineered for exhilaration

The Lexus IS delivers a refined balance of power and precision, designed to create a truly engaging driving experience.

  • Output: 311 horsepower
  • 0–60 mph: 5.9 seconds
  • Engine: 3.5L V6

Inside, the cabin features NuLuxe interior trim, race-inspired accents, and bolstered sport seats that reinforce its performance-driven character.

Performance at its purest

A true sport sedan begins with a powerful V6 and a responsive platform, enhanced by a finely tuned suspension and adaptive variable dampers that transform every road into an invitation to drive.
